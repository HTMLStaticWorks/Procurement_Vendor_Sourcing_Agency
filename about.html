Quality & Risk Governance

The 6-Stage Supplier Auditing Protocol

Fewer than 14% of applicant manufacturers meet Nexolve’s institutional standards. Every partner in our network is continuously benchmarked.

STAGE 01

Solvency & Financial Health

Deep balance sheet audit, working capital ratios, credit scores, and insurance coverage to eliminate supplier insolvency disruptions.

STAGE 02

ISO & Regulatory Compliance

Verification of active ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, CE, FDA, and RoHS certifications with direct registrar validation.

STAGE 03

On-Site Factory Audits

Physical plant inspection of tooling calibration, automated QA lines, cleanroom protocols, and material storage conditions.

STAGE 04

Sample Testing & Metrology

Independent CMM (Coordinate Measuring Machine) dimensional checks, tensile stress validation, and chemical composition assay.

STAGE 05

ESG & Labor Ethics Verification

SMETA / Sedex labor standard compliance, fair wage enforcement, zero child-labor guarantee, and Scope 1-3 carbon tracking.

STAGE 06

Binding SLA Execution

Contractual commitment to on-time delivery thresholds, defective-part replacement speed, and fixed indexation formulas.
